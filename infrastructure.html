The hearing record

The hearing record.

Zoning boards, plan commissions, and boards of appeals, several hundred times across twenty-three states. Three applications were denied. Two of those were decided politically rather than on the merits of the application.

A hearing is where every earlier shortcut becomes visible. The record is not a reflection of persuasive testimony; it is a reflection of what was resolved before anyone walked into the room: title clean, easements verified, ordinance read, staff consulted, findings prepared, objections anticipated.

Stated precisely: the denial count is exact: three. “Several hundred hearings” is a considered estimate rather than a counted figure, and it is not presented as anything firmer than that.

Source of the figures
