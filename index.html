What we build

Systems the people doing the work can run.

Operating systems, not tools

A tool solves one task and leaves the operation to hold the rest together. A system carries the whole month: what is true, who decides, what happens on an exception, and what evidence exists afterwards.

Seven are in daily production use at a property operator, covering expenses, property data, inventory, inspections and turnover, reached through one authenticated portal and run by a five-person team without a technical specialist.

Controls that hold when something goes wrong

Versioned packages, schema versioning, staged QA gates that block submission rather than warn about it, deployment preflight, and a rollback path prepared before the change goes in.

A field submission once returned an error after it had already committed. The transaction was identified by its submission ID and payload hash, confirmed, and recovered, instead of a user being told to try again and a duplicate entering a record people trust.

Web applications for the people doing the work

An authenticated employee portal behind Workspace sign-in. A field inspection application opened from a QR code at the property, which works offline, stages drafts on the device, and asks for sign-in only at submission. A private, invitation-only application built for my own family. A client intake and onboarding application is in build now.

Our approach

Context first. Tools second.

Complex operations rarely fail because a team lacks another tool. They fail when source information is scattered, responsibilities are unclear, field activity is hard to trace, and each handoff loses context. I organize those elements into usable operating systems, then applies automation and AI where they make the work more reliable.

I do not begin with a preferred software product. I begin with the people doing the work, the assets and records involved, the current source for each fact, the decisions and exceptions that matter, and the evidence needed to know the system worked. Tools are then selected or built around that operating model, and important actions remain visible, reviewable, and accountable to the people responsible for the outcome.
