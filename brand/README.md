# Brand assets

Artwork that is not a page and not a per-page share card, but has to stay in
step with `styles.css`. Kept here so the next person changing a brand colour
can find everything that colour appears in.

## linkedin-cover.png

LinkedIn profile banner, 1584x396. Josh's own drone photograph of a completed
compound, desaturated and washed to the brand ground.

Measured against `styles.css`, not eyeballed:

| Element | Measured | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Top rule | `rgb(204,230,106)`, 6px | `--lime` `#cce66a`, exact |
| Monogram disc | `rgb(204,230,106)` | `--lime`, exact |
| Headline rule | `rgb(204,230,106)` | `--lime`, exact |
| Ground | `rgb(22,35,33)` | `--ink` `#142221`, 2/1/0 off under the photo wash |
| Headline / wordmark ink | `rgb(244,241,233)` | `--paper` `#f4f1e9`, exact |

Contrast, computed rather than eyeballed, against the median ground and again
against the brightest patch of photograph behind each run:

| Text | vs median | vs brightest |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | 12.61 | — |
| Practice line, the tightest case | 8.34 | 6.84 |
| Headline | 12.56 | 9.30 |
| SSU monogram on lime | 11.79 | 4.28 |

All clear 4.5:1.

**No stat band, deliberately.** The figures already sit in the LinkedIn headline
directly beneath the banner, and Josh's objection to repeating them was "am I a
major league baseball player."

**If this is ever rebuilt:** the avatar overlays the bottom-left on desktop and
mobile crops the sides, so the empty left column below the wordmark is load
bearing, not an oversight.

The thirteen per-page share cards are generated by `tools/make-og.py` into
`og/`. This banner is hand-built and is not generated by that script.
